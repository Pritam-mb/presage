What the verifier can see

SOURCE-INDEPENDENT
  • Pulse value, SDK confidence and stability
  • Sample timing and validation transitions
  • Technical failures and trial completion
  • Optional response to a fresh blink prompt

Condition labels and filenames are never used by the decision rule. They are revealed only when grouping results.

What this cannot establish

  • Identity, consent authenticity or secure camera provenance
  • Resistance to real-time forged or injected video
  • Generalization across people, devices or lighting
  • A validated liveness detector or medical assessment

The blink check has only three possible prompts. Chance matches, facial-detection failures and accessibility limits must be reported.
