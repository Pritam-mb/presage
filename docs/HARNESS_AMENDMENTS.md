# Authorized harness amendments

The owner authorized the proposed corrections on November 15, 2026 with “do whatever u think right.” The original supplied code remains unchanged in `scripts/reference/`; `scripts/harness-runtime.cjs` is the active amended runner. Frozen measurement-policy thresholds are unchanged.

## Retained low-FPS attempts

G2.2 validates trial schema, processing consent and absence of forbidden identifiers. A low or unknown delivered frame rate is a measurement-quality limitation, not evidence that a genuine failed capture is a forged file. These attempts remain in the ledger, counts and original files. G2.3 reports their IDs explicitly as limited observations.

The separate real camera diagnostic must still deliver at least 25 FPS for at least six seconds. G3.2 now requires at least one completed live trial with signal output, at least 60 seconds, and saved delivered FPS at least 25. Retained limited attempts cannot establish that successful baseline. Missing FPS is never guessed from requested camera settings. Historical signal availability is not relabeled as quality-qualified success.

## Product-claim scan

The scan checks README, MVP, app source and Markdown product documents. Supplied developer references and executable tests are not product assertions. Diagnostic/diagnostics describe engineering output and are no longer matched by the overly broad `diagnos\w*` medical rule. Diagnosis and treatment claims remain prohibited, as do the original liveness/deepfake/security claims. Negated limitations remain allowed. The scan is a tripwire and still needs human review.

No failed trial was deleted, edited or promoted. No manual reviewer, public-use consent, video or publication was fabricated. MIT licensing applies to the source; it does not grant rights to private participant data or third-party recordings.

## Final human-review checks

Final mode additionally requires explicit checked M1-M5 attestations and a completed originality-review timestamp within 48 hours. Merely mentioning a competitor in a pending document does not complete that review. These checks record operator attestations, not automatic proof that a person watched a video or granted publication permission.
